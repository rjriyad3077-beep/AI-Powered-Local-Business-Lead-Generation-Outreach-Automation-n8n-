# AI-Powered Local Business Lead Generation & Outreach Automation (n8n)

This workflow automatically finds local business leads from Google Maps, crawls their websites, analyzes business data using AI, and generates personalized cold outreach messages ready for Google Sheets or CRM usage.

## Features

* Google Maps lead scraping
* Website content crawling
* AI-powered business analysis
* Personalized DM generation
* Google Sheets integration
* Automated lead processing loop
* Outreach-ready lead enrichment

## Workflow Process

1. Scrape local businesses from Google Maps
2. Store leads in Google Sheets
3. Crawl business websites
4. Extract website content
5. Analyze business data using OpenAI
6. Generate personalized outreach DMs
7. Save results back to Google Sheets

## Tech Stack

* n8n
* OpenAI
* Apify
* Google Sheets
* Google Maps Scraper
* Website Content Crawler
